## **Hidden Risks in Cell Tower Lease Contracts: What You Might Be Overlooking**

Signing a cell tower lease isn’t just about agreeing to rent out space-it’s about protecting your property rights and future financial potential. Unfortunately, many landowners don’t realize what they’re truly giving up until it’s too late.

Here are a few risks buried in the fine print:

- **Escalation clauses** that benefit carriers more than you, compounding over time.
- **Subleasing rights** that allow tenants to profit from your land-without sharing a cent with you.
- **Restrictions** that limit your ability to sell, build, or develop your own property in the future.

What seems like a straightforward agreement can actually be a long-term legal trap. Without a clear understanding of the language or leverage in negotiations, you could sign away far more than intended.

That’s why partnering with a seasoned Cell Tower Lease Consultant is crucial. They don’t just read the fine print-they interpret it with your best interests in mind, ensuring the deal you sign protects your land, your income, and your long-term freedom.
